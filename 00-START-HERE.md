# Start here

You've got a **Founder OS** — a way to run your business with an AI that's organised around your goals and does work between your sessions.

It's plain Markdown. The AI reads `CLAUDE.md` first (what the business is and what it's trying to do), then the file it needs, then it acts — drafting, reconciling, pressure-testing. It never sends anything or takes an irreversible action; you (founder, principal, owner, or creator — whoever runs the business) make every real call.

New to any of the words below? Keep `GLOSSARY.md` open in a tab.

---

## Step 0 — get set up (10 min)

1. **Get an AI app that can read a folder.** [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code) (a terminal app) or **Claude Desktop** both work. Point it at this folder.
2. **Type this as your first message:**
   > *"Open this folder and read CLAUDE.md, then tell me what you understand about my company."*
   That's it — the AI orients itself and you're off.
3. **Single machine? Not technical?** You can **skip every mention of git, push/pull, cron, and the two-machine setup** in these files. That's optional power-user stuff for people running routines on a spare always-on machine. The OS works perfectly on one laptop with none of it.

---

## Do these first

1. **Copy this folder** to where you keep your company's files, and rename it to your company. *(Technical users: fork it.)*
2. Fill in **`CLAUDE.md.template`** → save as `CLAUDE.md`. State the venture and, above all, your handful of current **objectives** — the system organises itself around those.
3. **Get it running:** wire up **one** routine from **`SCHEDULE.md`** (the weekly heartbeat is the place to start). Watch it for a week. Add the next only when it's earned its place.
4. **Then** skim **`SOURCES_OF_TRUTH.md`** and **`INGESTION_GATE.md`** — the trust foundation that stops the AI stating stale or unchecked facts as truth. (Each opens with a one-line plain-English summary.)

> These folders (`_crm/`, `_memory/`, `decisions/`, `research/`, `CONTROL_CENTRE.md`) ship as empty stubs — the AI fills them in as you go.

## The one rule

**Start with your goals and one routine — not ten.** The system is meant to stay small enough that you read everything it does. A handful of routines you actually watch beats fifty you ignore — and the whole point is that *you* stay the judgment, so the OS must never outgrow your ability to see what it's doing.

Once it's running, read **`ONGOING.md`** — how to live with it: what to connect, what context is worth keeping, and the weekly rhythm that keeps it sharp.
